# Credits

This skill is standalone and has no runtime dependency on other skills. The ideas below are credited to their sources, with values restated here so the skill works on its own.

- Decision gate by frequency and purpose, and the purpose taxonomy: the animation-building and opportunity-finding practice popularized by Emil Kowalski, including the essay "You Don't Need Animations".
- Specify exact properties rather than `all`, decelerate entrances, never scale from nothing, and grow popovers from their trigger: Emil Kowalski's design engineering philosophy.
- The `--motion-ease-out` and `--motion-ease-bounce` curves match the transitions.dev motion-token scale. The duration, distance, and scale values are a conventional set adapted for this skill, and the open and close asymmetry and stagger practice follow transitions.dev.
- The standard and accelerate easing curves: Material Design motion.
- Interruptible and redirectable motion, gesture feel, and cross-fade as the reduced-motion alternative: Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Designing Fluid Interfaces and Prefer Cross-Fade Transitions.
- Pause, stop, hide, and motion from interactions: WCAG 2.2 success criteria 2.2.2 and 2.3.3.
- Compositor-friendly properties and the 60fps budget: web performance guidance on animating `transform` and `opacity`.
- Baseline support levels: the web platform Baseline status for each feature.
